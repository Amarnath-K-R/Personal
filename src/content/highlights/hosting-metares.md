The event was led by Dr Gopal S. Pillai, Professor and Head of the Department, and Dr. Merin Dickson, Chief Research Officer of the Department. The theme was Connecting Minds, Creating Futures. It was a joy to see clinicians, researchers and students come together around one idea, that medical research should reach people.

We were honoured to have Dr. Mona Duggal, Director of the ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research), National Institute for Research in Digital Health and Shri. Suresh Gopi, Member of Parliament, Union Minister of State, as our chief guests. Their presence made the inauguration a memorable moment for the whole team.

One of the highlights for me was presenting my session, The AI of Oculomics, in front of highly esteemed dignitaries. They included Dr. Mona Duggal, Dr. P. Venkat Rangan, Vice Chancellor of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, and Dr. Anand Sivaraman, CEO and Founding Director of Remidio Innovative Solutions. Many other distinguished guests were also present. It was both an honour and a responsibility to speak before such a respected audience.

I began with the old Ayurvedic idea that the eye is a mirror of the body. I then showed how AI now helps us read that mirror with more care. I spoke about how a single retinal photograph, taken at a village camp with a handheld camera, may help flag people at risk of heart disease, stroke and kidney disease. I also shared how our models can run on a phone without an internet connection, so that screening can reach people who live far from a specialist. The talk was very well received, and I am thankful for the kind response.

After the session, Dr. Anand Sivaraman came to speak with me, told me that he enjoyed the talk and shared valuable feedback. He spoke about the importance of using clinical metadata along with retinal images, which can help detect these diseases with higher accuracy and make our models specific to each disease. This lets us answer, not only a simple question of disease or no disease, but go beyond these mere binaries. I am grateful for his time and his guidance. It was a real privilege to connect with him.

My sincere thanks to Dr Gopal S. Pillai and Dr. Merin Dickson for their leadership and trust. I also thank my colleagues in the Department of Ophthalmology, and every speaker, guest and participant who made this event meaningful.
