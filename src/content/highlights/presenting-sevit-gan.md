Looking back, one of the most memorable experiences of my research journey so far was presenting my work in Italy at ICICV 2025.

This was my first international conference where I had the opportunity to present a research paper, “SEViT-GAN: Attention based GAN with SE Residual blocks for Image Inpainting.” The work explored a hybrid approach combining Vision Transformer attention, Squeeze-and-Excitation blocks, and Generative Adversarial Networks for image inpainting.

But the experience was about much more than presenting a paper.

It was also my first time travelling outside India.

From experiencing a new university and meeting people from different backgrounds, to exploring the city, trying new food, and attending the conference dinner after the city tour, every part of the experience was something new.

The people at the University of Calabria were incredibly welcoming, and that made the whole experience even more memorable.

It reminded me that research is not only about the papers we write or the models we build. It is also about the people we meet, the places we discover, and the experiences that shape us along the way.

Grateful for this experience and for everyone who made my first international academic journey so special.

ICICV 2025 | University of Calabria, Italy | June 4 to 6, 2025
