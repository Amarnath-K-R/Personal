I received the Excellence in Ophthalmic Research and AI award from the Department of Ophthalmology, Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences, Kochi, at AIvishkar, Metares 3.0. The award was presented to me by Dr. Mona Duggal, Director of the ICMR National Institute for Research in Digital Health, New Delhi. As I stood there holding the award, my heart was full.

This award means more to me than I can say. It came from the clinicians I work beside every day. They see the patients, they ask the hard questions, and they keep this research honest. To be recognised by them, and to receive it from such a respected leader in digital health, feels like being told that the road I chose is the right one.

Research is a quiet path. Many days bring little visible progress, and it takes patience to keep going. There are long hours of sorting photographs, cleaning records and checking every detail. There are early results that are not strong enough. Each time, we had to go back, learn and try again, until more data and more care made the work better.

The road was not easy. Along the way, we faced hard problems. Biases in the data, differences between patients, and the many small challenges of real clinical work tested us again and again. There were moments when it felt like the answers would not come. But we worked through them, one by one. Where we stand today is because of resilience and patience, and because of a team that never gave up.

Over time, the work grew from photographs collected at camps, to models that learn, to a phone app that works without internet. A model should always show where it looked, so that a clinician can judge whether it makes sense. It must assist the doctor, and never replace the doctor. Every step reminded me that behind each photograph there is a person and a family.

The award ends with the line “Celebrating Innovation. Advancing Vision. Inspiring the Future.” These words touched me deeply. I see them as a promise. I want to keep learning, keep listening to clinicians, and bring screening closer to those who need it most.

From my heart, thank you to Dr. Mona Duggal for this honour. Thank you to Dr. Gopal S. Pillai and Dr. Merin Dickson for believing in me. Thank you to my colleagues in the Department of Ophthalmology, and to the patients who trusted us with their time. I am grateful beyond words, and I promise to keep working with all my heart.
