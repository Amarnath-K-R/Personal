My role involved analysing data from the Human Personalized Omics Profiling (HPOP) project and developing an interactive web application that allows readers to explore the visualizations and data behind the research. I wanted to help make the figures more than static images by giving readers a way to interact with the data and investigate the results for themselves. The application is available under the Data Visuals section of the HPOP database.

This experience would not have been the same without the guidance and support of the people who helped me along the way. I'm especially grateful to Dr. Michael Snyder, Stanford W. Ascherman Professor of Genetics and head of the Snyder Lab at Stanford, Dr. Ekanath Rangan at Stanford Hospital, and Dr. P. Venkat Rangan, Vice Chancellor of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, for their guidance and encouragement throughout my research journey.

Looking back, this publication represents more than a line on my CV. It reflects the value of collaborative research, the importance of making scientific data accessible, and the opportunity to contribute something meaningful while continuing to grow as a researcher. Grateful to everyone who made this experience possible.
